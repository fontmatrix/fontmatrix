The work is in the public domain, because its author died more than seventy
years ago and the term of protection has run out everywhere Fontmatrix is
distributed, or because it is an official text of a state, which the copyright
laws of Bulgaria and of the other countries concerned exclude from protection.

The file names the work and its author in samples/CREDITS, together with the
edition it was taken from. Nobody holds rights over the text itself; an edition
that adds work of its own — a translation, a commentary, a scholarly
transliteration — is listed under its own licence instead.
